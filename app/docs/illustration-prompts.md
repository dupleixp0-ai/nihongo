# Prompts d'illustration — hiragana

Ces prompts servent à générer **une image par histoire mnémotechnique**, avec l'outil d'images de ton choix
(par exemple Nano Banana). Les images de référence des caractères sont dans `glyphes-reference.zip`
(un PNG 1024 × 1024 par caractère, fond blanc).

## Avant de commencer
- Vérifie que les conditions d'utilisation de l'outil autorisent l'**usage commercial** des images générées.
- Ne fournis à l'outil **aucune image d'un tiers** (captures de vidéos, illustrations d'autres méthodes) comme référence.
- Garde une trace des prompts et des dates de génération.

## Mode d'emploi
1. Ouvre l'outil, joint le PNG du caractère (ex. `ka.png`).
2. Colle le **bloc de style**, puis le **prompt du caractère**.
3. **Contrôle la forme du caractère** : les générateurs la déforment parfois. Si elle n'est plus fidèle, régénère.
4. Exporte en PNG carré (1:1, au moins 1024 × 1024) et nomme le fichier d'après la transcription : `ka.png`.
5. Envoie-moi les images dans la conversation : je les ajoute au projet avec leur texte alternatif.

## Bloc de style (à coller à chaque fois)
Flat vector illustration, soft pastel palette on a light blue-grey background (#EEF1F5), main accent indigo (#2C4A8C),
secondary accents in sage green and soft coral used sparingly, clean simple shapes, friendly and calm mood, square 1:1
format, generous empty margins, absolutely no text, letters or numbers in the image except the attached character.
Use the attached image of the hiragana character as the base. Keep the character EXACTLY as it is (same shape, stroke
thickness, proportions and position). Do not redraw, distort, move or replace it. Build the illustration around and over
the character so that its strokes become parts of the picture, and keep the character clearly legible.

## Prompts par caractère

**あ (a) — l'ancre.** The horizontal stroke is the crossbar of a ship's anchor, the vertical stroke is its shaft,
and the big curved loop at the bottom is the anchor's curved hook. Add a small ring at the top and a rope, with a few
light waves behind.

**い (i) — deux souris.** Two cute cartoon mice sitting side by side, mouth open squeaking. The long curved left stroke
is the big mouse's body and tail, the short right stroke is the small mouse's tail. Add round ears, eyes and whiskers.

**う (u) — le toboggan.** The big curve is a playground slide. A smiling child sits at the top start of the curve about to
slide down, arms in the air, with a small ladder behind. The small stroke at the top is a little flag on the ladder.

**え (e) — l'éléphant.** A friendly baby elephant. The long curved stroke is its back and its trunk curling up at the
end. Add a big round ear, an eye and a little tusk. The short top stroke is a small tuft of hair on its head.

**お (o) — le bol et l'olive.** A bowl on a table. The horizontal stroke is the tabletop, the vertical stroke with the
loop is a round bowl, and the small stroke at the top right is a green olive with a red center falling into the bowl.

**か (ka) — le cactus fleuri.** A cactus. The long slanted stroke is the trunk, the horizontal stroke that crosses it and
curves down is one arm of the cactus, and the small stroke at the top right is a pink flower that has just opened.
Add a few small spines and a terracotta pot.

**き (ki) — le kimono.** A kimono seen from the front. The two horizontal strokes are the wide open sleeves, the
slanted stroke is the fold of fabric falling in the middle, and the curved stroke at the bottom is the knotted obi belt.
Add a subtle fabric pattern.

**く (ku) — le coude.** A bent arm in a sleeve. The single stroke is the arm: upper arm and forearm meeting at a sharp
angle, the elbow pointing left. Add a fabric sleeve and a small hand at the lower end.

**け (ke) — le quai et la canne à pêche.** A small harbor dock. The short left vertical stroke is a mooring post with a
rope. The horizontal stroke and the long vertical stroke that curls at the bottom form a fishing rod, with the line and a
small hook dipping into the water. Add a few waves and one small fish.

**こ (ko) — la bouche qui dit « co ».** A stylized friendly mouth. The short top stroke is the upper lip and the long
curved bottom stroke is the lower lip, shaped as if saying "co". Add soft pink lips color and a hint of cheeks.
